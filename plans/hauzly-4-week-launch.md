# @hauzlyai — 4-Week Instagram Launch Plan

**Window:** Mon Nov 2 – Sun Nov 29, 2026 · **Owner:** Josh Nova / Matt Melton · **Source brief:** `brand/hauzly-brief.md`

## The point
- **Goal:** 1,000 trial starts in 90 days ≈ **78/week**. Report **activated trials** too (first bill/invoice or appliance logged in week 1).
- **Instagram's job:** proof and destination. When a realtor, creator or founder sends someone to @hauzlyai, the grid has to answer "what is this, do I need it, how do I start?" in 10 seconds.
- **Volume comes from elsewhere:** realtor/mortgage partners (closing gift), creator collabs, founder accounts, small boosts on posts that already work.
- **Why now:** November = first freeze, furnace season, Thanksgiving hosting. Real, dated pain for all three audiences.

**Assumptions to confirm before posting**
- [CONFIRM] Aha moment = asking "when was the furnace last serviced?" and getting the answer with the invoice attached.
- [CONFIRM] Starter tier includes photo/forward/ask. (Emailing providers is Home tier and up. Don't show it in Starter demos.)
- ✅ Web app at hauzly.com (no app stores). 30-day trial, **no card**. Say "no card" in every conversion CTA. Ready for volume per founders.
- ✅ Junk-drawer origin story is true. Post 3 is a go.
- [CONFIRM] At least 2 beta users will give a quote with permission by Week 3.

## 5. Measurement

**Weekly scorecard** (every Sunday, log winners in `brand/what-worked.md`):

| Metric | Why |
|---|---|
| Reach, and % from non-followers | Is the content leaving our bubble? |
| Shares per 1K reach | Reach-post health; sends drive the algorithm |
| Saves ÷ reach | Trust/carousel health |
| Profile visits ÷ reach | Do hooks create curiosity about Hauzly? |
| Bio + Story link taps ÷ profile visits | Does the profile convert curiosity? |
| Trial starts **by utm_source** | Which channel actually drives volume |
| **Activated trials** ÷ trial starts | Quality. Below 40% [assumption] means fix onboarding before buying traffic |
| Cost per trial start (boosts only) | Week 4 paid test |

Skip follower count as a goal. Track it, but a follower who never taps the link costs us content time and gives nothing back.

**Week 2 funnel check (Sun Nov 15)**
1. Pull real rates from Weeks 1–2: visit rate (V) = profile visits ÷ reach, tap rate (T) = link taps ÷ visits, sign-up rate (S) = trials ÷ taps.
2. Reach needed for IG's share = IG trial target ÷ (V × T × S).
   Example: 1.5% × 15% × 30% = 0.0675%, so **~1.5M reach per 1,000 trials**. That's not doable organically from zero.
3. Working split [CONFIRM]: partners 50%, creators 20%, founders 10%, IG organic + boosts 20% (~200 trials ≈ 300K reach at the example rates).
4. Decide:
   - IG on pace (≥15 trials/week by Week 2): keep the mix, boost winners in Week 4.
   - Partners ahead of IG: move founder time from content to partner outreach. IG drops to 3 posts/week, all proof and demo.
   - Taps fine but sign-ups <20%: fix the hauzly.com mobile landing page and sign-up inside Instagram's in-app browser, not Instagram content.
   - Trials fine but activation low: fix onboarding (first-bill upload nudge) before spending more on traffic.
