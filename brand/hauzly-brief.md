## Product
- **One sentence:** Hauzly is your AI house manager — it organizes everything about your home (appliances, maintenance, service providers, expenses, projects) and answers questions about any of it in plain language.
- **The #1 pain it removes:** The mental load of homeownership. What you need to know about your home "lives in our heads, buried in emails, or scattered across drawers and folders." Receipts in a drawer, vendors in a text thread, paint colors forgotten.
- **Core idea / hook:** "What if your home had a memory?"
- **How it works (no manual entry):** Forward an invoice, snap a photo, or just tell it what happened — it logs it and remembers it. Setup = upload a utility bill or give your address.
- **Higher tiers:** Hauzly Home and up emails providers on your behalf and syncs to Google Calendar/Tasks. Family plan = multiple homes. Plans: Starter, Home, Family.
- **Brand assets:** Name HAUZLY; orange house-face mascot (built into the "u" of the wordmark).

## Audience
- **Three audiences** (tag every post with one — never write to all three at once):
  1. **First-time buyers** — never owned before. Don't know what they don't know. Pain: "nobody told me the house came with a to-do list." Trigger moment: closing.
  2. **Movers into a new home** — have owned before, new house. Know the drill, hate redoing it. Pain: rebuilding vendors, warranties, paperwork from zero. Trigger moment: move-in week.
  3. **Overwhelmed owners** — settled in, drowning in it. Pain: the mental load, receipts in drawers, "when did we last service the furnace?" No trigger moment — reach them through relatable everyday pain.
- **Priority:** Lead with 1 and 2. They have a dated trigger (closing/move-in) and a natural referrer (realtors, mortgage brokers, movers). Audience 3 is the long-term core but the hardest to reach cold.
